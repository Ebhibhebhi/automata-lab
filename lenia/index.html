3D Lenia Lab

Continuous 3D cellular automaton. FFT convolution runs in a background thread; rendering is GPU ray-marched.

Preset

Kernel K (who counts as a neighbour)

K(r): weight given to a cell at distance r. Each β entry is one concentric shell, with that peak height.

Growth G (how cells respond)

Curve: G(u), growth for neighbourhood value u = K∗A. Bars: where the live cells' u values actually fall right now (log scale). Cells grow where G > 0.

How it works

Each cell holds a value A in [0, 1]. Every step:
1. U = K ∗ A, a weighted average of the surrounding shell (computed with a 3D FFT).
2. G(U) = 2·exp(−(U−μ)²/2σ²) − 1, which is +1 when U ≈ μ and −1 far from it.
3. A ← clip(A + G(U)/T, 0, 1).

A creature (soliton) is a blob whose own shape keeps U near μ at its body and away from μ just outside. In 3D these are rare. The presets here were found by a short automated search, and they are only metastable: most eventually bud and fill space, or fade. Finding long-lived or moving 3D creatures is the open part.
