Notation

Rules are written S/B/C/N: survival counts, birth counts, number of states, neighbourhood (M or VN). Lists take ranges, e.g. 4-6,9.

A dead cell (state 0) is born when its alive-neighbour count is in B. An alive cell (state 1) stays alive if its count is in S. Otherwise, with C > 2 it starts decaying through states 2 to C-1, then dies. Decaying cells can't be reborn and don't count as neighbours. C = 2 is plain Life-like behaviour.

Bays' 3D Life notation EₗEᵤFₗFᵤ maps to Eₗ-Eᵤ / Fₗ-Fᵤ / 2 / M, so Life 4555 is 4-5/5/2/M.
